# Valmyndigheten

Researched: 2026-03-19

## What it is

Valmyndigheten publishes open, aggregated election data and raw election files.

## Access and licensing

- Open data is free to use.
- No agreement is required.
- Data is available only at aggregated level, not individual level.
- Valmyndigheten asks to be stated as the source.

## Formats and access

- Main open-data formats are `csv`.
- Other formats such as `txt`, `xlsx`, `xml`, and `json` may also be available.
- Data is published in as machine-readable a form as possible.
- Raw data is available for elections from 2006-2022 and newer election cycles.

## What makes Valmyndigheten attractive for the course

- Very clean join partner for municipality-level panels.
- Election turnout or party-result extensions are easy to motivate.
- Lower technical friction than many API sources.
- Good source for teaching about aggregation and public-data limits.

## What makes Valmyndigheten awkward

- The data is much cleaner than SCB or Kolada and therefore less useful as a primary wrangling source.
- The update frequency is tied to election cycles.
- It is best used as an optional enrichment source, not as a backbone.

## Provisional course role

Use as a clean optional join partner for municipal turnout or election-result extensions.
